How to Use Eduqii

Get started with your digital learning journey using these general steps. The exact options may vary depending on how your school or organization has set up Eduqii.

Getting started

  1. Open Eduqii. Use the link provided by your school, educator, or organization.
  2. Sign in or create your account. Follow your institution's instructions. If you have not received access details, ask your teacher or administrator.
  3. Find your learning materials. Once signed in, look for your assigned courses or learning activities.
  4. Work through each lesson. Read or watch the materials, and complete any quizzes, assignments, or activities included with the lesson.
  5. Check your progress. Use the course or progress area, if available, to review completed activities and see what to do next.

How-to guides

  1. Read the full question and identify its instruction words, such as explain, compare, or calculate.
  2. Pick out the key facts and decide what the question is asking you to do.
  3. Plan your answer. For calculations, show your working; for written answers, organize your points clearly.
  4. Answer every part, then check your work against the question before moving on.

  4. Answer every part and review your work, paying attention to the marks and any exam instructions.

Follow the timing and other exam guidance provided by your teacher.

  1. Open the question-creation area available to your account, or ask your teacher or administrator where to find it.
  2. Choose a question format and enter a clear question based on the lesson objective.
  3. Add answer choices or an expected answer, and mark the correct answer if the format requires it.
  4. Review the question for clarity, check any settings, and save or submit it using the available controls.

Having trouble?

  • If you cannot sign in, check that you are using the correct account details and the Eduqii link provided by your institution.
  • If a lesson or activity does not load, refresh the page and check your internet connection. You can also try another up-to-date browser.
  • For missing courses, access issues, or questions about assignments, contact your teacher or institution administrator.
